A LITTLE KNOWLEDGE ABOUT NIGERIA.

Population: Nigeria is the most populous nation in Africa and ranks among the top ten most populated countries globally.

Linguistic Diversity: While English serves as the official language, more than 500 distinct indigenous languages are spoken throughout the country, with Hausa, Yoruba, and Igbo being the largest.

Name Origin: The name "Nigeria" was derived from the Niger River, the longest river running through the nation.

Biodiversity: The country, particularly the tropical rainforest region around Cross River State, is renowned for hosting one of the richest diversities of butterflies on Earth.
Urban Scale: Lagos, Nigeria’s economic center and former capital, is one of the largest metropolitan regions in Africa, with a population exceeding 20 million residents.
